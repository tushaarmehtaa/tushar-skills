# Slashskills project guidance

For product, UX, information architecture, content placement, SEO, discovery, installation, or trust decisions, read [the product principles](workspace/product-principles.md).

Tushar designated https://www.skills.sh/ as our gold-standard benchmark alongside our own product judgment. Inspect the comparable current surface, understand the reader job, and explain meaningful departures rather than copying blindly.

The homepage is for finding and installing skills. Guides and editorial content belong on separate pages and contextual skill links; do not put a guide feed or promotion on the homepage.

Preserve unrelated worktree changes. Follow the repository's existing validation and publication contracts.
