## Follow-through: Codex catalog experiment

The approved catalog-budget experiment ran on Codex CLI 0.152.1 with `gpt-5.6-sol`. The final capture began with 109 visible skills; adding 100 project probes kept all 209 names visible but shortened probe descriptions to 40–44 characters and emitted Codex's catalog-pressure warning. An earlier 111-skill baseline produced the same project-description lengths and omission counts. Front-loaded implicit discovery succeeded 3/3 in the final opaque-name check; the equivalent rear trigger, shortened away from the catalog, succeeded 1/3 and twice selected an unrelated skill. Explicit invocation succeeded 3/3.

Decision: update `/guides/codex` and `/skill-creator`; publish no new URL. Do not claim a stable omission threshold or universal selection rate from this single environment and small behavioral sample.

Production follow-through on 2026-09-03: the updated `https://www.slashskills.xyz/guides/codex` returned HTTP 200 and exposed the new catalog-pressure heading, 118–120 and 40–44 character markers, a self-canonical URL, and `index, follow`.
