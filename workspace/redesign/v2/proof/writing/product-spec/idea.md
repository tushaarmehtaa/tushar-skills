# Idea: shared folders in Quillnote (synthetic sample)

Quillnote is a note-taking app (web + iOS). Today every note is private to one account. Pricing: Free, and Pro at $8/month.

Ask from sales + 3 of our top 20 accounts: "let me share a folder with my team so we all see the same meeting notes."

Rough thoughts from the founder:
- Owner shares a folder by email invite. Invitees can view or edit.
- Edits should show up for everyone. We do NOT have real-time collaborative editing today; notes save on blur and last write wins.
- Should invitees need Pro? Not sure. Sales wants it free for invitees to spread the product.
- Deleting a shared folder: what happens to other people's copies? Unclear.
- iOS app is 2 releases behind web; App Store review takes ~3 days.
- We want something in customers' hands within 6 weeks.
