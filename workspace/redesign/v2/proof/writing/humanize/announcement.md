# Ledgerly Sync is live

Today we're launching Ledgerly Sync. It connects your bank accounts, payment processors, and invoicing tools to Ledgerly, so you can see all of your finances in one place.

It works the same whether you're a solo freelancer or a team of fifteen:

- **Setup takes minutes.** Connect your accounts and you're done.
- **Your cash flow is current.** Data updates in real time, so you're never looking at last week's numbers.
- **Your data is encrypted.** We use bank-grade encryption.

Sync also categorizes transactions for you by matching them automatically. During the beta, 212 businesses had 94% of their transactions matched with no manual work.

One caveat: some Canadian bank connections still need a manual CSV upload. We're finishing that integration now.

Ledgerly Sync is available today on the Growth plan ($29/month). If you're on Starter, you can try it free for 30 days.
