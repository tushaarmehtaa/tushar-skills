Traction (as of Sept 2026):

- Market: ~4,000 independent bike shops in the US. Founders think Canada + UK adds ~2,500.

Money: raised $400k pre-seed from angels in 2025. $210k in the bank. Burn $28k/month. Default-alive date: not yet, ~7.5 months runway.

Plan for the raise: "$2.5M seed to hire 3 engineers + 2 salespeople and expand into outdoor/ski shops and UK." No investor list yet. Founders want to start meetings in January.

Open questions the founders have: Is this a venture-scale business? Should we raise at all, or get to profitability? What valuation is reasonable?
