I've rewritten the copy in `index.html`. It isn't committed and I haven't viewed it in a browser. Every claim comes from `product-notes.md`, and I didn't calculate any new numbers from the ones in there.

**Who the page is for:** managers of independent vet clinics (2–12 vets, 5–40 staff) who build the rota in a spreadsheet. The page asks them to start a 30-day trial with no card.

**Main claim:** Tidepool drafts your weekly rota from your coverage rules and fills sick calls by text, so Monday mornings aren't spent chasing cover. The headline uses the problem as managers describe it, and the testimonial says the same thing back to them.

**What changed**
- **Hero:** "Unlock the Power of Next-Generation Scheduling" became "Stop spending Monday morning texting people to cover sick calls." It now says who it's for (vet clinics) and how it works (draft rota, cover by text).
- **Removed false or vague claims:** "AI-powered", "Enterprise-grade security" and "thousands of happy customers" are gone. In their place:
  - an FAQ answer saying it's a constraint solver, not AI;
  - "SOC 2 Type I completed, Type II in progress";
  - "61 paying clinics in the US and Canada".
- **How it works:** the vague features list became three things the product actually does:
  - builds the rota from your coverage rules, using the "1 vet + 2 techs" example from the notes;
  - texts eligible staff in order of fewest hours, and the first YES gets the shift;
  - exports hours to payroll, with the limit stated: only Gusto and ADP today.
- **New objections section:** "My staff won't install another app" is answered with plain SMS. "We use our PIMS" is answered with "it never touches appointments".
- **Pricing:** "Contact us" became the real terms: $6 per staff member per month, billed monthly, $60 minimum, 30-day trial with no card.
- **Buttons:** "Get Started" became "Start your 30-day free trial", which tells people what they're signing up for.
