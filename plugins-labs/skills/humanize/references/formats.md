# Format register

Read only the section matching the deliverable. Format requirements outrank a generic preference for conversational prose.

## Social posts and threads

Protect brevity, fragments, lowercase, direct opinion, and native informality when they belong to the author. Remove unearned hooks, engagement-bait questions, templated lesson lists, and one-line staccato used through the entire post.

## Newsletters and personal essays

Protect digressions, uneven paragraphs, jokes, and recurring personal habits that create the relationship with the reader. Remove generic greetings, unnecessary roadmaps, repetitive recaps, and endings that inflate a specific observation into universal wisdom.

## Reported or analytical essays

Protect the argument's necessary setup, sourced uncertainty, long sentences doing real logical work, and terminology the subject requires. Remove duplicated conclusions, vague authority, ornamental significance, and metaphors that replace the mechanism.

## Landing and marketing copy

Preserve product nouns, concise declaratives, and repeated terminology. Test whether a claim could be pasted onto several unrelated competitors. Replace generic benefit language only with capabilities, evidence, or outcomes supported by the source material.

## Documentation and READMEs

Prioritize a working outcome, exact terminology, scannable structure, code or tables where they reduce ambiguity, and explicit responsibility. Passive voice may be correct when the actor is the system or irrelevant. Do not inject marketing cadence or personality into procedural reference material.

## Interface copy

Match the user's state and the system's actual knowledge. Preserve concise controls and conventional labels when they are clearest. Never make errors, destructive actions, payment consequences, privacy choices, or permissions playful merely to sound human.

## Cold outreach

Protect directness and a specific observation that proves attention. Remove reusable flattery, sender autobiography before relevance, and asks buried under politeness. Do not invent personalization.

## Video and spoken scripts

Read the revision aloud. Protect useful repetition, breathing room, fragments, and conversational transitions. Remove written constructions that trip the tongue or retention hooks that promise more than the script delivers.

## Legal, medical, financial, and policy text

Do not trade accuracy, mandated language, provenance, or calibrated uncertainty for warmth. Flag ambiguous or inaccessible language, but require appropriate subject-matter review before changing consequential meaning.
