Fieldwork: who buys, when, and the next 90 days

Built from 5 real deals and 1 paid workshop. As of 12 Oct 2026.

Independent vet clinics with 2-5 vets, in the 60 days after opening a second site or losing their bookkeeper.

Who
Owner-run veterinary clinics with 8-25 staff and $1-4M in revenue, still on spreadsheets or a part-time bookkeeper
When
A second location opens, a partner buys in, the bookkeeper quits, or a tax deadline is missed
Buyer
The owner-vet, or a practice manager who brings the owner into the call
Replaces
A part-time in-house bookkeeper and the owner's Sunday-night spreadsheets
Not a fit
Corporate-owned clinics; no change coming in the next 60 days; only a front-desk contact; owners who won't share read-only bank access

PatternsFour findings decide the ICP; each traces to specific deals.
  1. Owners buy; managers scope.Both retainers had the owner on a call. Pinecrest stayed a one-off.
  2. Triggers beat budgets.Both retainers began within 30 days of a trigger. Both losses had none.
  3. Corporate ownership kills the deal.Valley Vet Corp could not approve anything locally.
  4. Every retainer started with a cleanup project.The fixed-price project removed the risk of a first yes.
