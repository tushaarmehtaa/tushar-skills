# Single-message examples

Adapt these structures; never copy facts, names, claims, or tone that do not belong to the sender.

## Prospect with a verified operational signal

```text
Subject: [company]’s [specific change]

Hi [Name] — saw [verified signal]. It usually creates [relevant operational consequence].

We helped [credible comparable context] achieve [verified result] by [brief mechanism]. Would it be useful if I sent the two-page breakdown?

[Name]
```

Use only when the consequence is a reasonable inference. Otherwise ask a question instead of asserting it.

## Advice request

```text
Subject: your decision on [specific topic]

Hi [Name] — your explanation of [specific work] changed how I’m thinking about [concrete decision].

I’m choosing between [A] and [B]. If you have a minute: what failure mode would you examine first?

[Name]
```

This keeps the request answerable without requiring a meeting.

## Investor message

```text
Subject: [company] — [round or concrete wedge]

Hi [Name] — your work with [relevant verified investment/thesis] made me think this may fit.

[Company] helps [customer] [outcome] through [mechanism]. [One verified traction or learning claim]. We’re raising [round/amount] to reach [specific milestone].

Open to a short overview?

[Name]
```

Do not cite a portfolio company without checking current involvement and conflicts.

## Introduction request

```text
Hi [Connector] — would you be comfortable introducing me to [Recipient] about [specific reason]?

Why it may be relevant to them: [one sentence]. Here is a forwardable note below. No worries if the fit or timing is unclear.
```

Give the connector a genuine opt-out and a complete forwardable note.

## Confirmation notes kept outside copy

```text
Confirm before sending:
- source and date for the opening signal;
- permission to name the customer or mutual contact;
- accuracy and time period of the result;
- sender identity, opt-out, and jurisdiction requirements.
```
